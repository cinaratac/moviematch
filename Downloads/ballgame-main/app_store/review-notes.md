# App Review notes

Meteors VS Planets is a single-player precision arcade game.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No real-money purchase or in-app purchase is available.
- The Shop uses coins earned only through gameplay.
- Interstitial ads may appear after selected level unlocks.
- On iOS, the tracking permission prompt is used for advertising attribution/personalization. The game remains playable if permission is denied.
- All levels can be reviewed without external hardware or a backend account.

Suggested review path:

1. Tap to launch the moving ball toward a blue target.
2. Avoid red targets and the red ring.
3. Open Menu to inspect level selection.
4. Open Tutorial for gameplay instructions.
5. Open Shop to inspect coin-only cosmetic items.
