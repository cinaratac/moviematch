# Privacy Policy for Meteors VS Planets

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Çınar Atakul ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Meteors VS Planets mobile application.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores level progress, earned coins, owned cosmetic items, and the selected cosmetic item locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or receive this game-progress data on our servers.

## Advertising and information collected by partners

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your approximate location derived from your IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, interactions with the app or ads, crash data, and performance data. They may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, subject to your consent and device settings.

On iOS, the app may ask for permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites. Denying this permission does not prevent you from playing the game. You can change this permission later in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Consent choices

Where required by law, an advertising consent message may be shown before ads are requested. You can also use the privacy controls made available by your device and by Google.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can address the request.

## Data retention

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and your consent choices.

## International processing

Advertising partners may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, our partners, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
