## Description

Timing is everything in Meteors VS Planets, a fast and focused arcade challenge set in deep space.

Launch at the perfect moment, hit the right targets, and avoid deadly red hazards. Each level adds a fresh twist with changing orbit patterns, moving targets, dangerous rings, and precision challenges that test your reactions.

FEATURES

• 90 handcrafted level variations
• Simple one-touch controls
• Increasingly challenging orbit patterns
• Coins and unlockable arrow styles earned through play
• Built-in tutorial and level selection
• Fast sessions with instant restarts
• Offline gameplay; internet is only used to request ads

Can you master every orbit?
